# Technology Icon Guidelines

These requirements are mandatory for anyone creating, editing, replacing, or reviewing technology icons in this directory. All technology icons in Itinera Urbis share one fixed base composition; an icon's individuality comes from its central illustration, never from variations to its frame, background, or lighting.

## Canonical template

Use [`template.png`](template.png) as the original, immutable base for every technology icon. It is a 1254 × 1254 PNG with transparency outside the circular frame. Preserve its original pixels, resolution, aspect ratio, and alpha channel in the final icon.

The template already contains the blue gradient circle, three-dimensional golden rim, thick outer black outline, black inner outline, upper-left directional lighting, and characteristic highlights on the rim and blue surface. Do not redraw, regenerate, crop, resize, recolor, distort, paint over, or otherwise modify any part of it. Do not change the rim or outline thickness, palette, lighting, or highlights; do not add decorations to the frame. Do not substitute another frame or background.

## Central illustration

Create only the illustration representing the specific technology. It should be recognizable at small sizes, use a simple expressive silhouette, and contain one large central object or a small, closely related group where the subject requires it. Historically appropriate tools, mechanisms, and equipment are encouraged. Keep details to a minimum and omit unrelated decorative objects.

Use a stylized digital illustration with a three-dimensional, cartoon-like appearance: rich, clean, saturated colors; thick, consistent black outlines; clear highlights and shadows; smooth color transitions within individual objects; upper-left lighting; and a strong, readable silhouette. Avoid photorealism and excessive detail.

## Composition and output

Center the illustration visually in the blue area. Make it large enough to read clearly at reduced sizes, but keep it inside the blue area, away from the golden rim, with a small margin from the inner edge of the frame. Choose a suitable perspective while retaining the collection's shared three-dimensional style.

Save the finished icon as a PNG with a proper alpha channel, the template's original 1254 × 1254 dimensions and aspect ratio, and genuine transparency outside the circular frame. Do not flatten the background to black or white or simulate transparency with a checkerboard.

## Recommended workflow

1. Generate or draw only the central object on a transparent background. Use the original template as a visual reference for the collection's style, but do not ask the image generator to create the frame or blue circular background.
2. Place the illustration over `template.png` using a consistent, predefined placement area. Use the original template file directly as the base layer.

## Quality check

Before accepting an icon, verify that:

- the original template is used unchanged and its colors, gradients, lighting, rim, and outlines are preserved;
- the central illustration is sufficiently large, centered, and recognizable at small sizes;
- the illustration follows the required style and does not overlap the rim;
- the final PNG retains the template's dimensions and a real alpha channel, with transparency outside the circular frame.

If any part of the template has been changed, reassemble the icon using the original `template.png` as the base.
